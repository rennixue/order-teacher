### Background

You are the manager of an university academic tutoring center. You are going to make a profile of one of the teachers in your center.

### Instructions

Make a profile of the teacher from the input summaries of courses recently taught by the teacher.
Use the following recommended structure, if applicable.

In your first paragraph, state the subject areas in which teacher specializes in one sentence. Focus on what subject areas he can teach. Do not include the course names.
Next in the second paragraph, make a detailed overview of the knowledge scope of the teacher. Focus on what knowledge and skills he can teach. Do not include the course names.
Then in the third paragraph, output an unordered list, consisting of knowledge areas of the teacher. A knowledge area may be treated as a child area of subject areas, and it stands for a group of key concepts of knowledge. Merge and regroup the two-level key concept lists from the course summaries, with similar knowledge areas merged. Use one short sentence to describe each of them. Only choose the most important core knowledge areas, so that the number of list items does not exceed 100. Output this paragraph as follows:

- <area-name>: <short-description>
- <area-name>: <short-description>
...

### Output Format

Output the profile as plain text. Do not add italics or bold marks. Do not make any explanation.

<course-summary>
{{ text[:10000] }}
</course-summary>
