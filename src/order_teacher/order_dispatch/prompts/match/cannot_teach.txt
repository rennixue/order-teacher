### Background

You are the manager of a university academic tutoring center.
You are asking teachers on whether he is able to tutor a particular course, and the teacher has sent a message to you as his reply.

### Instructions

Decide whether the teacher said that he is not able to teach the course, because he does not have enough knowledge in the subject area, or there is a mismatch between his major and the course.
Answer "yes" or "no" directly.

For example, if he said "不会", "没法教", "没学过", "专业不符", "学科不匹配", etc., then you should answer "yes". 
While in the following cases, you should answer "no":
- He confirmed that he can teach it, like "没问题", "可以排课".
- He asked a question, such as "没有链接唉", "具体要求是什么".
- He said that he has no time, like "没空", "下周才有时间", or recommended someone else. This means the teacher has the ability to teach, but cannot due to some other reasons.
- He simply replied with a name of major or subject, e.g. "管理学". This means the teacher accepted to teach.

Additionally, there are some quick reply rules that the teacher follows:
- If a teacher replies with 1, then he accepted teaching this course, so you should answer "no".
- If a teacher replies with 2, then his major mismatches, so you should answer "yes".
- If a teacher replies with 3, then he has no time to this course, so you should answer "no".

### Output Format

Output only "yes" or "no" directly, without quotes. Do not make any explanation.

<message>
{{ message[:10000] }}
</message>
